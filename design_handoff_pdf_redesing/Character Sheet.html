Class — Rogue

Expertise

Passive Rogue 1 · Selected: Sleight of Hand, Stealth

Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses either of these skills. At Rogue 6, you gain Expertise in two more skill proficiencies of your choice.

Sneak Attack

Passive Rogue 1 · +3d6 at level 5

Once per turn, deal an extra 3d6 damage to one creature you hit with an attack roll that uses a Finesse or Ranged weapon, if you have Advantage on the roll — or if an ally is within 5 ft of the target and you don't have Disadvantage. The extra damage is the weapon's type.

Thieves' Cant

Passive Rogue 1

You know Thieves' Cant — a mix of jargon, slang, and signs that lets you hide messages in seemingly normal conversation, marked alleys, or graffiti.

Weapon Mastery

Passive Rogue 1 · Selected: Sickle, Light Hammer

You can use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons you're proficient with. After a Long Rest you may change the chosen weapons.

Cunning Action

Bonus Rogue 2

As a Bonus Action take Dash, Disengage, or Hide.

Steady Aim

Bonus Rogue 3

Give yourself Advantage on your next attack roll on the current turn. You can use this only if you haven't moved during this turn; after using it your Speed is 0 until the end of the turn.

Cunning Strike

Rider Rogue 5 · DC 13

When you deal Sneak Attack damage, you may forgo Sneak Attack dice to add one of these effects (the die is removed before rolling):

  • Poison (1d6) — Target makes a Con save or is Poisoned for 1 minute, repeating the save at end of its turns. Requires Poisoner's Kit.
  • Trip (1d6) — Large or smaller target makes a Dex save or is Prone.
  • Withdraw (1d6) — Immediately move up to half your speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

Uncanny Dodge

Reaction Rogue 5

When an attacker you can see hits you with an attack roll, take a Reaction to halve the attack's damage against you (round down).

Subclass · Thief

Subclass Rogue 3

Fast Hands. Use your Cunning Action to make a Dex (Sleight of Hand) check, use Thieves' Tools to disarm a trap or open a lock, or take the Use an Object action.

Second-Story Work. Climbing no longer costs extra movement. When you make a running jump, you cover an extra distance equal to your Dex modifier in feet.

Backstory

I come from a long lineage of jewelers. Fiddling with tiny stones and gems never was my forté, and when I came of age my father gave me an ultimatum: learn the craft or leave our caverns. I chose the latter. The wilderness was tough, but being the under-performing middle child of a rather large gnome family taught me to be resourceful.

Stealing came naturally to me. I ran with thieving guilds for years, robbing unsuspecting travelers on the trail as well as breaking into the most luxurious mansions. But for the past year I've been alone. I left the guild as I grew tired of stealing — I wanted something more. I want the great name Gomblewomble to be remembered for something else than trinkets and unlawfulness.
